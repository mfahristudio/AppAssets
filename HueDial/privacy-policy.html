Information Collection and Use

HueDial is designed as a localized offline gameplay experience. We do not set up accounts, log in credentials, or directly collect or store personal identifying information (PII) such as names, addresses, phone numbers, or emails on our servers.

However, to provide advertisements, in-app reviews, and gameplay analysis, the App utilizes trusted third-party SDKs that may collect device-level identifiers automatically. The service providers we use and their policies include:

AdMob Integration & Advertising Identifiers

We integrate Google AdMob to serve App Open, Interstitial, and Rewarded advertisements. AdMob uses advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or Google Advertising ID) and device metadata to display relevant, non-intrusive ads, prevent ad fraud, and track impressions.

These advertising identifiers are unique, resettable IDs provided by your device's operating system. You can reset these identifiers or opt out of personalized tracking at any time through your device's settings menu:

  • Open Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization or Reset advertising ID.

Local Playtime and Score Storage

The App saves your gameplay data, including difficulty selections, average scores, and cumulative playtime, locally on your device via SharedPreferences. This data remains on your physical device, is not transmitted to us, and is only used to run the game, track round progress, and trigger the native 10-minute In-App Review prompt.

Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us. Since the game stores your gameplay history locally on your device, the protection of this data relies heavily on your device's built-in operating system security. We use secure Android API methods to write and read these local preferences.

Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our records. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we will be able to perform the necessary actions.

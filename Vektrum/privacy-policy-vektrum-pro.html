Vektrum Pro Package: com.synth.vektrum

Privacy Policy

Developer: M.Fahri Studio | Effective Date: October 27, 2026

Welcome to Vektrum Pro, the professional full-version analog vector synthesizer application designed and developed by M.Fahri Studio. We take the privacy of musicians and creators seriously. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, device permissions, and the protection of your creative work.

Core Privacy Commitment: Vektrum Pro is a private, offline musical workstation. We do NOT harvest, collect, store, monitor, or sell your personal data, audio recordings, musical compositions, or location.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Vektrum Pro is built with a privacy-by-design architecture:

  • No User Accounts or Telemetry: You do not need to register, provide an email address, or log in to use Vektrum Pro.
  • No Cloud Recording Storage: When you record your live synth performances as WAV audio files, the recordings are stored strictly locally on your device's internal storage. They are never uploaded to any remote server or third-party service without your direct manual action (such as using Android's system share sheet).
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect your name, phone number, contacts, financial info, or hardware identifiers.
  • No Commercial Advertising or Cross-App Tracking: Vektrum Pro contains zero advertisements and zero tracking SDKs.

2. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

Vektrum Pro requests minimal device permissions strictly needed for audio production and MIDI connectivity:

BLUETOOTH / BLUETOOTH_CONNECT / BLUETOOTH_SCAN

Purpose: Enables bidirectional communication with external wireless Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) MIDI keyboards, hardware synthesizers, and foot controllers. On Android 12 and above, the neverForLocation flag is explicitly enforced.

ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION (Android 10 & 11 only)

Purpose: Legacy requirement mandated by Android OS 10 and 11 to initiate Bluetooth LE scanning. Vektrum Pro does never access, record, or track your physical GPS or network location.

LOCAL STORAGE & FILE ACCESS (Android Storage Access Framework)

Purpose: Allows you to save your recorded WAV files, export custom synthesizer sound patches as JSON files, and import user sound libraries. All file operations are initiated strictly by your manual input via standard Android file choosers.

3. Third-Party Libraries & System Services

Vektrum Pro uses official Google Play system libraries to guarantee official software distribution, security, and update delivery:

  • Google Play In-App Updates (Play Core): Facilitates seamless background downloads of bug fixes and sound engine performance improvements directly from the Google Play Store.
  • Google Play In-App Review: Allows users to rate and review Vektrum Pro on the Google Play Store through a native system dialog.
  • Google Play Integrity API: Verifies that the application binary is unmodified, authentic, and legitimately licensed from the Google Play Store to protect against unauthorized tampered builds. This check operates without transmitting personal identifying information.
  • Google Oboe Audio: An open-source C++ audio library developed by Google to achieve ultra-low audio latency directly through Android's AAudio and OpenSL ES sub-systems. Oboe operates 100% locally on your device's audio hardware.

For more details on how Google processes system diagnostics, please consult Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Offline Performance & Data Retention

Vektrum Pro is designed for live stage performances and studio sessions without an active internet connection. All custom presets, live sets, audio recordings, and tuning microtonal configurations remain permanently stored on your device until you decide to delete or export them. We maintain no external database or server copy of your data.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Vektrum Pro is safe for users of all ages, including children and students. We do not knowingly collect, harvest, or request personal data from anyone under the age of 13. If you believe any personal information has inadvertently been provided to us, please contact us immediately for prompt resolution.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to accommodate future feature additions or legal requirements. Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Effective Date".

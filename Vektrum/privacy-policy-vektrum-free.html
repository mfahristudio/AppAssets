Vektrum Free Package: com.synth.vektrum.free

Privacy Policy

Developer: M.Fahri Studio | Effective Date: October 27, 2026

Welcome to Vektrum Free, a high-performance analog vector synthesizer application designed and developed by M.Fahri Studio. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application.

Core Privacy Commitment: Vektrum Free is designed as a standalone musical instrument. We do NOT collect, harvest, store, or sell any personal information, location data, or audio data from our users.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike many online applications, Vektrum Free operates primarily offline as an on-device digital synthesizer:

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any email address or personal credentials to use Vektrum Free.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect your name, phone number, address, contacts, or financial details.
  • No Audio or Sound Transmission: All sound generation and real-time DSP computation take place entirely on your device via the native C++ Oboe audio engine. Your audio output is never transmitted to any external server.
  • No Advertising Trackers: Vektrum Free contains no third-party advertising frameworks (such as AdMob, Unity Ads, or Meta Audience Network) and does not track you across other apps or websites.

2. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

Vektrum Free requests only the minimal system permissions necessary to provide its musical performance functionality:

BLUETOOTH / BLUETOOTH_CONNECT / BLUETOOTH_SCAN

Purpose: Used exclusively to connect and communicate with wireless Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) MIDI keyboards, controllers, and musical instruments. On Android 12+, this permission includes the neverForLocation flag.

ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION (Android 10 & 11 only)

Purpose: Required strictly by older Android operating system APIs (Android 10 and 11) to discover nearby Bluetooth Low Energy MIDI devices. Vektrum Free does never access, track, record, or share your geographic location.

3. Third-Party Libraries & Services

Vektrum Free uses a limited number of official Google Play system libraries to ensure app reliability and delivery of updates:

  • Google Play In-App Updates (Play Core): Facilitates automatic background downloading of bug fixes and performance updates directly from the official Google Play Store.
  • Google Play In-App Review: Allows users to optionally rate and review the app on the Google Play Store using Google's native dialog prompt without leaving the app.
  • Google Oboe Audio: An open-source C++ audio library developed by Google to provide low-latency audio rendering directly through OpenSL ES and AAudio. Oboe runs completely offline on your device hardware.

These libraries may process standard device diagnostics (such as OS version and device model) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Vektrum Free is safe for users of all ages, including children. Because we do not collect, request, or store any personal data from any user, we comply fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly ensure no such data exists.

5. Upgrading to Vektrum Pro

Vektrum Free contains buttons that provide informational dialogs regarding optional upgrade to Vektrum Pro. When tapped, these buttons direct you via standard Android intent to the official Google Play Store listing of Vektrum Pro. No payment information or financial transactions are processed inside Vektrum Free.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements or app features. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy from time to time.

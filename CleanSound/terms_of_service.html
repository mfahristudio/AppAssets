2. Description of the Application

Clean Sound is a utility tool designed to help remove water droplets, moisture, and fine dust particles from mobile device speaker grills by generating acoustic frequencies, sinusoidal sound waves, sonic vibrations, and tailored pulse intervals. The App features:

  • Auto Mode: Automated multi-frequency sound sweep sequence with synchronized vibration cycles.
  • Manual Mode: Fine-tuned frequency selection slider (ranging between low-end and high-end sonic spectrum) with optional pulse modulation.
  • Sound Diagnostic Test: Independent channel audio checks (Left channel, Right channel, and Stereo audio output).
  • Quick Settings Tile: Quick activation from Android system notifications shade.

3. Crucial Hearing, Health & Acoustic Safety Warning

IMPORTANT HEALTH & SAFETY NOTICE:

The Application operates by generating high-intensity, concentrated audio frequencies and maximum volume output to physically oscillate the device's internal speaker diaphragm and eject trapped liquids.

  • DO NOT hold the mobile device directly against your ear or facial area while the cleaning process is executing.
  • DO NOT use this Application while wearing headphones, earphones, earbuds, or Bluetooth audio headsets connected to the device. Immediate acoustic trauma or permanent hearing impairment could occur if high frequencies are blasted into enclosed in-ear canals.
  • Keep the device out of reach of infants, small children, and pets during active cleaning operations.
  • Place the device flat on a stable surface with the speaker facing downwards, allowing expelled liquids to escape unimpeded.

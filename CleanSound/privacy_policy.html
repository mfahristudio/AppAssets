2. Data Collection & Usage

A. Information We Do NOT Collect

Clean Sound is designed to function locally on your device without collecting personal data. We do NOT collect:

  • Personal identifiers (such as your legal name, home address, personal phone number, or government ID).
  • Account credentials (the App requires no signup, login, or social authentication).
  • Microphone or audio recordings (the App generates outgoing speaker sound waves; it does not record audio).
  • Contacts, photo gallery, media files, or external documents.
  • Precise GPS location data.
  • Financial, payment, or credit card details.

B. Technical Data Collected by Third-Party SDKs

To provide the app free of charge and ensure stability, the App integrates third-party services that may automatically collect non-personal, pseudonymized technical information as described below:

  • Device Information: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, and system language.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID), subject to your device settings and consent preferences.
  • Usage & Performance Metrics: Ad impressions, ad clicks, interaction events, and crash logs to maintain app reliability.
  • IP Address: Processed automatically by advertising and network infrastructure to determine coarse geographic location (such as country or region) for regulatory compliance and ad serving.

3. Android Device Permissions

The App requests only the minimum required Android permissions strictly necessary for its features to operate:

Permission Technical Identifier Purpose & Justification
Vibrate android.permission.VIBRATE Synchronizes the device's physical vibration motor with sonic frequency pulses to agitate and dislodge trapped moisture and dust from the speaker mesh.
Modify Audio Settings android.permission.MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS Allows the App to maximize playback volume during the cleaning cycle and route acoustic frequencies between the Main Loudspeaker and the Earpiece speaker. Restores your volume afterward.
Internet Access android.permission.INTERNET Enables loading third-party advertisements via Google AdMob and checking regulatory privacy consent updates via Google UMP SDK.
Network State android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Allows the App to detect network connectivity before initiating ad requests, preventing unnecessary battery and data drain when offline.
